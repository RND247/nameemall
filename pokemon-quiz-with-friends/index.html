Pokémon Quiz with Friends

Who knows more Pokémon, you or your friends? Create a room, share the link, and play together in real time. No accounts and no downloads.

In ⚔️ Duel you race to name Pokémon before your opponent does. In 🤝 Co-Op you work as a team to fill the whole Pokédex together.

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Create a Duel or Co-Op room and send the link to a friend.
  • Pick which generations to play before you start.
  • Every name you type shows up on both screens instantly.
  • For a solo competition, send a Daily challenge link and compare scores.

FAQ

How do I play the Pokémon quiz with a friend?

Open the Multiplayer tab, choose Duel or Co-Op, and share the room link. Your friend joins straight from the browser.

Do we need to be on the same network?

No. You can play with friends anywhere; the link connects you directly.

How many players can join?

Duel and Co-Op are designed for two players. For bigger groups, everyone can play the same Daily and compare results.
