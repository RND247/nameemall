Gen 2 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 100 Johto Pokémon

Generation 2 brought 100 new Pokémon to the Johto region in Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal (1999). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Johto Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #152 Chikorita to #251 Celebi, including the starters Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile and legendaries like Lugia, Ho-Oh and Celebi.

▶ Start the Gen 2 quiz

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Gen 2 Pokémon are there?

There are 100 Pokémon in Generation 2 (Johto), numbered #152 to #251 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 2 starters?

The Johto starter Pokémon are Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile.

What is a good score on the Gen 2 quiz?

Naming half of the 100 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Gold, Silver and Crystal often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 100 in a few minutes.
