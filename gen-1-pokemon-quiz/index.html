Gen 1 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 151 Kanto Pokémon

Generation 1 brought 151 new Pokémon to the Kanto region in Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow (1996). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Kanto Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #1 Bulbasaur to #151 Mew, including the starters Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle and legendaries like Mewtwo and Mew.

▶ Start the Gen 1 quiz

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Gen 1 Pokémon are there?

There are 151 Pokémon in Generation 1 (Kanto), numbered #1 to #151 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 1 starters?

The Kanto starter Pokémon are Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle.

What is a good score on the Gen 1 quiz?

Naming half of the 151 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Red, Blue and Yellow often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 151 in a few minutes.
