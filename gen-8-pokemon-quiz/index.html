Gen 8 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 96 Galar Pokémon

Generation 8 brought 96 new Pokémon to the Galar region in Pokémon Sword and Shield (2019). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Galar Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #810 Grookey to #905 Enamorus, including the starters Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble and legendaries like Zacian, Zamazenta and Eternatus.

▶ Start the Gen 8 quiz

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Gen 8 Pokémon are there?

There are 96 Pokémon in Generation 8 (Galar), numbered #810 to #905 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 8 starters?

The Galar starter Pokémon are Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble.

What is a good score on the Gen 8 quiz?

Naming half of the 96 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Sword and Shield often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 96 in a few minutes.
