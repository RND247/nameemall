FAQ

How many Gen 6 Pokémon are there?

There are 72 Pokémon in Generation 6 (Kalos), numbered #650 to #721 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 6 starters?

The Kalos starter Pokémon are Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie.

What is a good score on the Gen 6 quiz?

Naming half of the 72 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with X and Y often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 72 in a few minutes.
