There are 127 Pokémon with the Grass type. How many can you name from memory, from Bulbasaur to Hydrapple?

Type a name and its card fills in. Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter, and you can switch to Japanese, Chinese, Korean, French, German, Spanish or Italian names.

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Type a Pokémon that isn't part of this quiz and the game tells you, so you can move on.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Grass-type Pokémon are there?

This quiz has 127. Every Pokémon whose main form has the Grass type, as one of its one or two types. Regional forms are not counted separately.

Is the quiz free?

Yes. NameEmAll is free, runs in the browser and needs no account.

Can I play other themes?

Yes. Open ⚙️ Settings in the game and pick another theme from Themed quizzes, or choose one below.
