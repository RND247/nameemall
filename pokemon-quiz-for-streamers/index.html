  • Horizontal (16:9) or vertical (9:16): in vertical mode the game stays inside a fixed 9:16 frame, so cropping in OBS or your editor takes seconds.

FAQ

How do I turn on Creator Mode?

Open ⚙️ Settings in the Full Pokédex and choose 🎥 Creator Mode, or use the button on this page. Your choice is remembered until you press Exit.

Can I use NameEmAll on stream or in videos?

Yes. NameEmAll is free to play on stream and in videos. A link to nameemall.com in your description is appreciated.

Does it work with OBS?

Yes. Capture the browser window in OBS, Streamlabs or any recorder. In vertical mode, crop the capture to the 9:16 game frame.

Can my Twitch chat play along?

Yes. Type your channel name in the Creator Mode setup. Chat messages count as guesses, with no login or Twitch app needed. Choose Together to fill the Pokédex as a team, or Chat vs You to race your viewers.
