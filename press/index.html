Press Kit: NameEmAll for Streamers

NameEmAll is a free browser game: name all 1025 Pokémon from memory. With Twitch Chat Plays, your viewers type Pokémon names in chat and fill the Pokédex with you, or against you.

▶ Set up Creator Mode

Free to use on stream and in videos, no permission needed. A link to nameemall.com in your description is appreciated.

Set it up in 30 seconds

  1. Open nameemall.com/?creator=setup (or ⚙️ Settings → 🎥 Creator Mode).
  2. Choose horizontal (YouTube, Twitch) or vertical (TikTok, Shorts), and type your Twitch channel name.
  3. Pick 🤝 Together or ⚔️ Chat vs You, press Start, and capture the browser window in OBS.

What your stream gets

💬 Twitch Chat PlaysViewers name Pokémon by typing them in your chat. No login, no bot, read-only.⚔️ Chat vs YouRace your chat. Great for a quick segment with chat comparing scores.
