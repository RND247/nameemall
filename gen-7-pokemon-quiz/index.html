Generation 7 brought 88 new Pokémon to the Alola region in Pokémon Sun and Moon (2016). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Alola Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #722 Rowlet to #809 Melmetal, including the starters Rowlet, Litten and Popplio and legendaries like Solgaleo, Lunala and Necrozma.

FAQ

How many Gen 7 Pokémon are there?

There are 88 Pokémon in Generation 7 (Alola), numbered #722 to #809 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 7 starters?

The Alola starter Pokémon are Rowlet, Litten and Popplio.

What is a good score on the Gen 7 quiz?

Naming half of the 88 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Sun and Moon often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 88 in a few minutes.
