Gen 4 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 107 Sinnoh Pokémon

Generation 4 brought 107 new Pokémon to the Sinnoh region in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl and Platinum (2006). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Sinnoh Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #387 Turtwig to #493 Arceus, including the starters Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup and legendaries like Dialga, Palkia and Arceus.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Gen 4 Pokémon are there?

There are 107 Pokémon in Generation 4 (Sinnoh), numbered #387 to #493 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 4 starters?

The Sinnoh starter Pokémon are Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup.

What is a good score on the Gen 4 quiz?

Naming half of the 107 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Diamond, Pearl and Platinum often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 107 in a few minutes.
