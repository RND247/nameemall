Gen 9 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 120 Paldea Pokémon

Generation 9 brought 120 new Pokémon to the Paldea region in Pokémon Scarlet and Violet (2022). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Paldea Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #906 Sprigatito to #1025 Pecharunt, including the starters Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly and legendaries like Koraidon, Miraidon and Terapagos.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
