Gen 5 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 156 Unova Pokémon

Generation 5 brought 156 new Pokémon to the Unova region in Pokémon Black and White (2010). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Unova Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #494 Victini to #649 Genesect, including the starters Snivy, Tepig and Oshawott and legendaries like Reshiram, Zekrom and Kyurem.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.

FAQ

How many Gen 5 Pokémon are there?

There are 156 Pokémon in Generation 5 (Unova), numbered #494 to #649 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 5 starters?

The Unova starter Pokémon are Snivy, Tepig and Oshawott.

What is a good score on the Gen 5 quiz?

Naming half of the 156 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Black and White often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 156 in a few minutes.
