# What's new in NameEmAll

Player-facing release notes. When a version ships, the section whose heading matches
version.txt is posted to the Discord #announcements channel automatically
(.github/workflows/discord-release.yml). No section for a version means no post, so small
fixes can ship quietly. Keep it short, plain and in players' words.
To attach screenshots, add lines like "Image: press/screenshot.png" (a file in this repo, up to 4).

## v1.21.12

- Every skin has been redesigned from scratch. Each one now has its own background, its own tiles, its own animation when you name a Pokémon, and a signature moment:
- 🪨 Fossils: stone slabs crack open, and finished regions glow amber
- 🪶 Legendary Birds: a feather wipes the frost off, and quick streaks bring lightning, embers and ice
- 🌀 Ultra Beasts: neon tiles glitch in, and the wormhole pulses on combos
- ⏳ Creation Trio: cards collapse into a singularity, and space-time warps every 100 names
- 🫧 Mythicals: bubbles pop, found cards turn holo, and mythicals get a rainbow outline
- 📓 Starters: leaves, embers or bubbles by type, and a gold stamp on every finished starter line
- ✨ Eeveelutions: an evolution flash in the matching color, and a parade when you name all 9
- 🍬 New Rare Candy skin, a thank-you for Ko-fi supporters: "+1 Lv" on every name and a Level up banner every 100
- Also new: 简体中文 and עברית (Hebrew, fully right-to-left), so NameEmAll now speaks 10 languages

## v1.20.0

- Your Full Pokédex run now waits for you: switch to the Daily or Multiplayer and come back to find it paused right where you left it
- When a run ends, a "Start a new run" button takes the place of the name box
- The browser's Back button and #daily links now switch views properly
- Typed a near miss like "gyrados" and pressed Enter? A tip now points you to the ✏️ Spelling Assistant

## v1.19.0

- Creator Mode: a live combo meter now sits at the bottom of the screen. Name Pokémon within 5 seconds of each other to keep it going, and watch the bar drain. Every 5 in a row it changes color with a burst, all the way up to a rainbow 🌈 Ultra combo at 25

## v1.18.0

- New: skins! Each Collections achievement unlocks a look for the whole game, starting with Eeveelutions: Eevee's family in your header, a glow in its colors and sparkles every time you name a Pokémon
- 7 skins to collect: Eeveelutions, Starters, Legendary Birds, Creation Trio, Fossils, Mythicals and Ultra Beasts. Pick yours in ⚙️ Settings, where locked ones show how close you are
- The Spotlight Pokémon no longer has a shadow behind it

## v1.17.0

- Achievements now open in a window on top of your game, so you can check your progress mid-game without ending it. Close it with ✕, Esc or a tap outside
- Joining a Duel or Co-Op room that has closed or is already full now tells you so, instead of waiting forever

## v1.16.0

- Share your achievements: every unlocked achievement now has a 📤 button that makes a picture card, and "Share my achievements" shows your whole collection
- Show off in the new #achievements channel, and find opponents in #find-a-duel
- "Compare results on our Discord" now copies your Daily result, ready to paste

## v1.15.1

- NameEmAll has a Discord! Finish a Daily and tap "Compare results on our Discord" to join

## v1.15.0

- New 🎯 Quiz button: pick generations, order and themed quizzes in one place, and see what you're playing at a glance
- Themed quizzes are now cards: 8 themes plus all 18 types
- 🌐 Language has its own button, and Settings is much shorter
- On phones, menus slide up from the bottom

## v1.14.2

- Fixed Reset and Give Up not working in the installed app

## v1.14.0

- Install NameEmAll as an app on your phone or computer, and play offline
- The 3 Gen 10 starters are here (turn on Gen 10 in Generations)
- 26 themed quizzes: starters, legendaries, Eeveelutions, every type and more
