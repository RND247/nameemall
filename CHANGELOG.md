# What's new in NameEmAll

Player-facing release notes. When a version ships, the section whose heading matches
version.txt is posted to the Discord #announcements channel automatically
(.github/workflows/discord-release.yml). No section for a version means no post, so small
fixes can ship quietly. Keep it short, plain and in players' words.

## v1.15.1

- NameEmAll has a Discord! Finish a Daily and tap "Compare results on our Discord" to join

## v1.15.0

- New 🎯 Quiz button: pick generations, order and themed quizzes in one place, and see what you're playing at a glance
- Themed quizzes are now cards: 8 themes plus all 18 types
- 🌐 Language has its own button, and Settings is much shorter
- On phones, menus slide up from the bottom

## v1.14.2

- Fixed Reset and Give Up not working in the installed app

## v1.14.0

- Install NameEmAll as an app on your phone or computer, and play offline
- The 3 Gen 10 starters are here (turn on Gen 10 in Generations)
- 26 themed quizzes: starters, legendaries, Eeveelutions, every type and more
