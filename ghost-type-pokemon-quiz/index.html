FAQ

How many Ghost-type Pokémon are there?

This quiz has 65. Every Pokémon whose main form has the Ghost type, as one of its one or two types. Regional forms are not counted separately.

Is the quiz free?

Yes. NameEmAll is free, runs in the browser and needs no account.

Can I play other themes?

Yes. Open ⚙️ Settings in the game and pick another theme from Themed quizzes, or choose one below.
