Gen 3 Pokémon Quiz: Name All 135 Hoenn Pokémon

Generation 3 brought 135 new Pokémon to the Hoenn region in Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald (2002). How many of them can you name from memory?

Type any Hoenn Pokémon name and its card fills in. The quiz covers every Pokémon from #252 Treecko to #386 Deoxys, including the starters Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip and legendaries like Groudon, Kyogre and Rayquaza.

▶ Start the Gen 3 quiz

Free, no sign-up, works on phone and desktop. Also in 日本語, 繁體中文, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Italiano.

How it works

  • Start typing: there is no Enter key, a correct name is accepted the moment it is complete.
  • Names are forgiving: accents, capital letters and spaces don't matter.
  • Stuck? Turn on 💡 Hint to show silhouettes (your score is marked as played with hints).
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back and finish later.

FAQ

How many Gen 3 Pokémon are there?

There are 135 Pokémon in Generation 3 (Hoenn), numbered #252 to #386 in the National Pokédex.

Who are the Gen 3 starters?

The Hoenn starter Pokémon are Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip.

What is a good score on the Gen 3 quiz?

Naming half of the 135 Pokémon is a solid start. Fans who grew up with Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald often get 90% or more, and the best players finish all 135 in a few minutes.
